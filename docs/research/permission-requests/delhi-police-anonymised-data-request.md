**Subject:** Request for an anonymised, geographically generalised research extract of registered crimes against women, for an academic project

To: [Joint Commissioner of Police (Operations) / office to be confirmed]
Delhi Police Headquarters, Jai Singh Road, New Delhi 110001

Dear Sir or Madam,

I am [name], a [programme] student at [institution], working under the supervision of [supervisor name, designation].

SafeHer is a non-commercial academic project. It presents officially registered crimes against women in Delhi on an area-based map, with:
- full source attribution;
- clear statements of precision;
- no personal information.

It does not rank areas or produce safety scores. Its current figures come from NCRB's published district tables.

I write to ask whether Delhi Police could provide **a research dataset suitable for an academic or student project**: an anonymised and geographically generalised extract of registered cases. If such a dataset exists, or if there is a procedure for research requests, I would be grateful to be directed to it.

**Fields requested, generalised as Delhi Police considers appropriate:**

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| Anonymised record identifier | Not the FIR number |
| Original offence head, act and section | As recorded, with IPC, BNS or another act stated |
| Year and month of registration | Month only if permitted; otherwise year |
| Generalised location | A locality or an approximately 1 km grid-cell identifier, as Delhi Police chooses |
| Police district | |
| Police station | Only if appropriate, with small counts suppressed |
| Location precision and derivation | **How each location was derived:**<br>• the FIR place of occurrence;<br>• the caller's location;<br>• the police station;<br>• manual plotting;<br>• another method.<br>SafeHer would display only locations derived from the place of occurrence |
| Data dictionary | Definitions of every field and code |
| Record counts and coverage period | Total records by year and district, and the dates covered |
| Missing-data and location-quality documentation | For example, the share of records without a usable location, and any known limitations |

**Equally welcome instead of records:** aggregated counts by police district or police station, month and offence head.

**What we do not request:**
- names, phone numbers or addresses of any person;
- victim or complainant details;
- accused identities;
- FIR copies;
- FIR narrative or brief text;
- exact coordinates of residences;
- any other personal information.

Sexual offences and POCSO cases would be shown only as aggregated area counts, with small numbers suppressed, consistent with BNS s.72 and *Nipun Saxena v Union of India*.

**Questions we would also be grateful to have answered:**
1. Under what terms may the extract be **displayed publicly** in a non-commercial web map? Please also state the required attribution. We seek **written permission** for that visualisation.
2. Can Delhi Police confirm the **effective dates of the 16 police-district boundaries**? Do they correspond to the jurisdictions used in NCRB's district tables for 2020–2022?

We would be glad to share the display with your office before publication, and to correct or withdraw it on request.

Yours faithfully,
