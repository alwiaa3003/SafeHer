## Conclusion

**Verified against an official NCRB file: Delhi, 2019 = 13,395 registered cases.** A printed copy of Table 3A.1 in NCRB's own hosting has still not been obtained.

**Production update (10 November 2026):** after independent re-verification and your approval, Delhi UT 2019 = 13,395 was added to production with status `verified_with_note` and TOT-4 pending.

- A fresh download from the recorded NCRB URL was byte-identical to the held copy. The `ncrb.gov.in` URL redirects to `ncrb.mha.gov.in`, and the server reports the file as last modified on 25 March 2023.
- The worksheet "Total Cognizable SLL Crimes" holds static values: R38 = 51, S38 = 13,344 and T38 = 13,395.
- The only footnote is A43, "Source: Crime in India".
- The 2019 gap record is kept with status `resolved`.

| Question | Finding |

| Footnotes or definition changes? | The copy's note marks Jammu & Kashmir data for 2019 as including Ladakh. No footnote affecting Delhi was found. |

## Caution about revisions

The all-India 2019 total differs between sources: 405,861 in the official spreadsheet and 405,326 in the Crime in India 2021 copy. NCRB therefore revised some 2019 figures, or the two sources were compiled differently, but Delhi's value is identical in both. The cause of the all-India difference was not established.
